AI Flight School · Change program8

Managing resistance

Six objections we will hear, and how we answer them

“Will AI replace my job?”
Leaders say up front how freed time will be used: more projects and new roles such as Expert and Champion.
“I don't have time to learn.”
Protected learning time of 2 hours a week during each wave, approved by the manager.
“I don't trust AI answers.”
TRACE makes every answer traceable, and high-risk outputs always get human review.
“It's just another tool.”
Labs use real drone projects, and each team sees a quick win within 30 days.
“I'm not technical.”
Low-code tools, VinVed Copilot and role-based paths, with a champion on every team.
“Compliance will slow us down.”
Risk tiers mean low-risk use cases are approved in days, not weeks.

AI Flight School · Change program20

AI policies

Five policies every employee knows and signs

AI acceptable useApproved tools only. Client imagery and data never go into unapproved public AI tools. Signed every year.
Aerial data classificationPublic, internal, client-confidential and restricted. Restricted covers faces, license plates and critical infrastructure.
Human in the loopSets what AI may do alone and what needs a person's review or sign-off, by risk tier.
Model and output qualityAccuracy thresholds, drift monitoring and a rollback plan for every model and agent in production.
AI incident reportingReport errors, data leaks or misuse within 24 hours. Reviews are no-blame and feed back into training.
