Problem 01 of 06 · Business problem and outcome

Data Overload & Slow Post-Flight Processing

Business problem
  • Imagery, LiDAR and telemetry arrive in volumes teams can't absorb
  • Analysts spend 60–80% of their time on data prep and manual review
  • Ingest, clean and blend steps are manual and vary by analyst
  • Insights lag days to weeks after the flight
  • Throughput grows only by adding headcount
VinVed + AI solution
  • VinVed workflows automate ingestion, cleansing and transformation
  • Blend flight data with weather, GIS, ERP, CRM and asset registers
  • Spatial tools for geospatial joins and change analysis
  • In-workflow LLMs classify and summarize notes and metadata
  • Scheduled, repeatable runs on every new flight
Business outcome
  • Flight-to-insight in hours instead of days or weeks
  • More projects per analyst without proportional hiring
  • Consistent, repeatable outputs across teams
  • Lower storage and processing waste
  • Capacity to scale inspection, survey and ag contracts

Problem 02 of 06 · Business problem and outcome

Untrusted AI & Computer Vision Outcomes

Business problem
  • Defect, change and crop models trained on noisy, imbalanced data
  • Detection quality varies by site, sensor and analyst
  • Findings lack lineage, so clients and regulators push back
  • False positives waste truck rolls; misses create liability
  • AI stays stuck in pilot
VinVed + AI solution
  • Governed data prep and feature engineering for AI-ready datasets
  • VinVed Agent Studio turns approved data and logic into explainable agents
  • TRACE: Transparent, Repeatable, Auditable, Clear, Enforced
  • Full audit trail on every transformation and answer
  • VinVed MCP Server lets external AI tools call the same trusted logic
Business outcome
  • Higher, more consistent detection accuracy
  • Up to 70%+ less manual inspection review (industry-cited)
  • Fewer false positives and missed defects
  • Defensible AI findings for clients and regulators
  • Models move from pilot to production

Problem 04 of 06 · Business problem and outcome

Operational Inefficiency & Predictive Gaps

Business problem
  • Fleet maintenance is reactive; failures cancel missions
  • Flight plans don't optimize battery, fuel or coverage
  • Pilot and crew utilization is hard to see
  • Forecasts and cost allocation sit apart from financials
  • Pricing rests on gut feel, not data
VinVed + AI solution
  • Blend telemetry, maintenance logs, weather and financials
  • AutoML or custom models predict component failure
  • Automated recurring workflows for utilization and cost
  • Agents answer: “Which assets are highest risk this quarter?”
  • Root-cause analysis on delayed projects
Business outcome
  • Less unplanned downtime, lower maintenance cost
  • Optimized flights: battery, fuel and coverage
  • Better capacity planning and crew scheduling
  • Data-driven pricing and service levels
  • Improved margins and scalability

Business outcome
  • Confident, controlled AI adoption
  • Lower risk of non-compliance or untrusted decisions
  • Faster security reviews and contract qualification
  • Clear data ownership and access policy
  • Foundation for autonomous and agentic systems
